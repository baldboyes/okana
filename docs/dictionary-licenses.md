# Dictionary attribution and redistribution

Keep this notice, the source links, record provenance and visible credits with redistributed dictionary subsets. The generated data is an extraction/reordering of dictionary material, not new translations. Application code and practice answer data are not assigned the dictionaries' copyright by this notice.

The full multilingual JMdict build matches headwords against non-English dictionaries and adds their glosses as separate senses; languages are not sense-aligned. The downloaded XML headers have no per-gloss compiler/source/licence assignments. Attribution to HISPADIC/RUI is at compilation level, not a claim that a specific compiler authored every gloss. The original-source permission is interpreted using the project's explicit reference to WWWJDIC's non-English-source documentation and its subset-publication notice; CC BY-SA 3.0 is **not** represented as a blanket relicensing of the complete multilingual JMdict file. No Dutch material, dictionary images, dictionary stroke-order diagrams or example sentences are used. Separately imported Japanese AnimCJK stroke graphics are under the Arphic Public License, not dictionary licences; see [stroke graphics provenance and redistribution](kanji-strokes-sources.md) and the unaltered `public/kanji-strokes/ARPHICPL.TXT` and `COPYING.txt`.

## Publication obligations

1. Credit the dictionary projects, Spanish compilers and source links, including existing Kanjikana level-list credit, at the end of Opciones. Do not move credits to the modal.
2. Preserve the applicable attribution and share-alike conditions for dictionary-derived material, provide the licence links and retain this notice with the generated subset.
3. Identify extraction changes: selected target glyphs; Spanish-only glosses; pairing/sense restrictions; deterministic priority ordering; deduplication; maximum 20 words; joined headings/glosses. No translation was fabricated.
4. Do not claim ownership of the original dictionary text or blanket EDRDG ownership of Spanish translations. Additional redistribution of whole source files or unrelated compilations requires checking its own licence scope.
5. When updating, reread source notices and headers; stop if licence terms, attribution or distribution permissions change. For a distribution policy requiring a compiler-specific direct licence for raw Spanish JMdict extraction (rather than the project's linked subset-publication notice), obtain explicit HISPADIC/RUI/EDRDG confirmation before that distribution; the present XML alone cannot supply it.

## Evidence checked

The official EDRDG licence and WWWJDIC information pages were downloaded successfully and inspected. WWWJDIC's page reports updated 30 March 2025. Its Spanish source description lists approximately 41,000 combined JSPANISH entries; the JMdict project describes approximately 39,000 Spanish entries in its matching multilingual build. These are documentation totals, not this application's measured coverage. [Import/update procedure and actual counts](kanji-details-sources.md) records the checksums and observed source versions.
